About Ask Cloud Wizard

Ask Cloud Wizard is designed to make complicated purchase decisions easier to understand.

We organize publicly available information into plain-English comparisons, questions to ask and practical next steps. Our first topic is home power and energy, including backup generators, batteries and solar + storage.

Ask Cloud Wizard may earn compensation when users choose certain providers through links or referrals on the site. Those relationships will be disclosed, and we aim to distinguish informational comparisons from sponsored or compensated placements.

We are not an installer, contractor, utility, engineer, financial adviser or manufacturer. Site content is general educational information.
