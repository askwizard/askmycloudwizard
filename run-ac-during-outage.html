How can I keep central AC running during a power outage?

Central air is one of the largest electrical loads in many homes. The question is not simply whether a generator or battery can power an AC—it is whether the complete system is sized for your equipment's running and startup demands.

Three common approaches

Standby generator

Often selected when long-duration whole-home backup is the priority and a suitable fuel supply is available.

Home battery

Quiet and automatic. Running large HVAC loads may require more battery capacity or load management.

Solar + battery

Adds the possibility of daytime recharging during an extended outage, subject to system design and solar conditions.

Have these details ready

  • AC or heat-pump model and size
  • Electrical panel/service size
  • Whether you need one AC unit or multiple units
  • Other essential loads: refrigerator, lights, medical equipment, internet, well/pool pumps, etc.
  • Desired backup duration
Safety: generator and battery installations interact with a home's electrical system. Transfer equipment, permitting, ventilation and installation requirements should be handled according to applicable codes and manufacturer requirements.
