Generator vs. Battery vs. Solar + Battery

The best backup-power option depends less on the label and more on what you need to run, for how long, and whether you value backup only or everyday energy savings too.

Quick orientation: generators are designed for extended backup when fuel is available; batteries are quiet and automatic but have finite stored energy; solar + battery can recharge from sunlight and may provide everyday energy value as well as outage protection.
FactorStandby generatorHome batterySolar + battery

Questions to answer before requesting quotes

  • Do you need the entire house powered or only critical circuits?
  • Must central air conditioning operate during an outage?
  • How long do outages in your area typically last?
  • Do you have natural gas service?
  • Are you interested only in emergency backup, or also in reducing normal electricity purchases?
