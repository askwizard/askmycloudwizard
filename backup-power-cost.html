What does whole-home backup power actually cost?

Equipment price is only part of the quote. Compare the full installed system and its ongoing costs rather than comparing headline equipment prices.

Compare these quote components

Ongoing costsFuel + maintenanceLimited routine maintenance; eventual equipment replacementSystem maintenance considerations; eventual equipment replacement

Why quotes can differ so much

Home size alone does not determine price. Desired loads, existing electrical service, equipment capacity, installation complexity, local permitting, fuel availability, roof conditions and incentives can materially change the project.

Before accepting a quote

Ask the provider to separate equipment, installation/site work, permits, supplemental electrical/fuel work, maintenance and any optional items. That makes multiple quotes much easier to compare.
